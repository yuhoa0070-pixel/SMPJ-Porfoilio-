FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A quick interview about my experience, approach, and how I work with teams.

I’m a UX/UI designer based in Phnom Penh. I turn complex product ideas into clear, accessible digital experiences through research, user flows, interface design, prototyping, and design systems.

I have experience shaping websites, mobile products, dashboards, and scalable component systems. I’m comfortable contributing from early discovery through polished UI and developer handoff.

My core strengths are simplifying complex flows, building consistent visual systems, creating high-fidelity prototypes, and balancing user needs with practical business and technical constraints.

I involve teammates early, explain the reasoning behind decisions, document important states, and keep handoff practical. I value direct feedback and treat design as a shared product responsibility.

Figma is my main tool for interface design, components, prototypes, and handoff. I also use research, documentation, motion, and AI-assisted tools when they help the team learn or execute faster.

I separate personal preference from product evidence, ask questions when feedback is unclear, and adapt quickly when priorities change. I keep decisions visible so the team stays aligned.

I combine thoughtful UX reasoning with strong visual execution and practical collaboration. I can take ownership, communicate clearly, and help a team move from an unclear problem to a product people understand.
